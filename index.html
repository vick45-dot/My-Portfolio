About me

I’m Victor Kipkemboi, a freelance developer based in Nakuru. I’m in Year 2 of a Diploma in Information Technology at Kabarak University’s City Campus, and I take on real projects alongside my classes.

A lot of what I build deals with money and rules: stock and cash shifts in DawaTrack, deposits and deadlines in DreamPath, sales in a point-of-sale system. M-Pesa is part of most of it.

I learn by typing the code out myself. This year I rebuilt a personal site from scratch, line by line, to understand HTML and CSS properly instead of copying them in.

I speak English and Kiswahili. When I’m not building, I read novels, articles and magazines.
