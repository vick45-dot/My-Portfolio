About Nakuru KMTC

Shaping Kenya's Healthcare Workforce Since 1973

Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC) Nakuru Campus is one of the 71 KMTC colleges across Kenya. Nestled within Milimani Estate, the college has grown into a leading medical training hub producing skilled, compassionate healthcare professionals who serve communities across the country and beyond.

The campus's co-location with Nakuru County Teaching and Referral Hospital offers students unmatched hands-on clinical exposure, bridging the gap between theory and real-world medical practice from day one.

52-acre shared campus with county referral hospital
Two intakes per year — March and September
Online application through kmtc.ac.ke
State-of-the-art laboratories and simulation centres

Admissions at Nakuru KMTC

How to Apply

1
Visit KMTC Website

Go to kmtc.ac.ke and navigate to the online application portal.

2
Create an Account

Register with your KCSE index number and personal details.

3
Select Programme

Choose Nakuru Campus and your preferred health programme.

4
Submit Documents

Upload KCSE certificate, ID/birth certificate, and passport photo.

5
Await Placement

Check the portal for your selection and reporting instructions.

Intake Dates

March IntakeApplications: Nov — Jan
September IntakeApplications: May — Jul

Entry Requirements

  • Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE)
  • Minimum mean grade of C (plain) for diploma programmes
  • Strong grades in Biology, Chemistry and English
  • Certificate of Good Conduct
  • Medical fitness certificate

Fees Overview

Tuition fees are government-subsidised. Students may apply for HELB loans and bursaries.
