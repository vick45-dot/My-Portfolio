Who We Are

Welcome to St Francis Girls Suwerwa

St Francis Girls' School Suwerwa is a top-performing secondary school located in Kitale, Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. The school is well-known for its academic and co-curricular excellence, offering a vibrant, stimulating learning environment that prioritises the holistic development of students.

The school provides a rigorous academic programme that prepares students for success in higher education and beyond, with highly qualified teaching staff who use modern techniques to make learning engaging, interactive, and memorable.

With a well-established library, equipped classrooms, and modern facilities, students at SFG Suwerwa are assured of quality learning experiences rooted in Christian values.

Our Foundation

Embodiment of Hope

St Francis Girls Suwerwa anchors all its endeavors on the fear of the Lord and Christian values. The Board of Directors, school administration, staff, students and all stakeholders put God first in their day-to-day activities.

The exceptionally outstanding performance that the school has posted for years on end can be attributed to committed, highly-trained staff, as well as disciplined and focused students. We give glory and honour to God Almighty for this achievement.
